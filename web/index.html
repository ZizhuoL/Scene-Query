One real scene. Traceable evidence.

SceneQuery trains a randomly initialized neural radiance field on 48 public RGB-D views from TUM's Freiburg office sequence. Three separate views guide development; nine final views test reconstruction. Three controlled 6,000-step experiments run on the local M2 Max GPU.

The displayed surface is a point proxy sampled from learned geometry and appearance. A separate production Gaussian model trains on 525 observations with a temporal guard around every held-out image. The original fitted RGB-D surfels remain available as a comparison and supply stable search anchors in the same world coordinates. Open Training evidence for held-out predictions, errors and the depth-loss ablation.

OWLv2 is a frozen, pretrained image/text detector; its features are cached and associated with visible 3D points. Semantic caching and fusion are not training.

Similarity scores are not confidence probabilities. Missing depth, motion blur, occlusion and similar objects can produce incomplete or incorrect regions. This app does not discover hidden objects or perform general spatial reasoning.
